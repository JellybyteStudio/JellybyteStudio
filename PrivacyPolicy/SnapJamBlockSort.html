Privacy Policy

App: Snap Jam: Block Sort
Effective Date: November 17, 2026
Contact Email: JellybyteStudio@protonmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Snap Jam: Block Sort ("the Game", "we", "our") handles information when you play on Android devices. We do not operate custom game servers or sell personal data.

1. Local Data Storage

Your level progress, high score, virtual coins, lives, booster inventory, selected profile avatar/border, display nickname, and audio/vibration preferences are stored locally on your device using Godot local storage (user://save_data.json). You can clear this data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling the Game.

2. Third-Party Services Used

To keep the Game free to play, monitor stability, and support leaderboards and optional purchases, we integrate the following Google services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to display optional Rewarded Video Ads and frequency-capped Interstitial Ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Analytics & Firebase Crashlytics (Analytics & Stability): Used to collect anonymized gameplay telemetry (such as session starts, level completions/failures, scores, and ad events) and crash stack traces so we can fix bugs and balance levels. (Firebase Privacy & Security)
  • Google Play Games Services (Leaderboards & Achievements): When signed in, submits your high scores and milestone achievements to Google Play Games.
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): Processes optional in-app purchases (such as Remove Ads and booster/cosmetic bundles) securely through Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details.

3. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Local save data remains on your device until you uninstall the Game. Diagnostic and analytics logs held by Firebase and AdMob follow Google's standard retention schedules. If you would like assistance requesting deletion of any associated data or have privacy questions, email us at JellybyteStudio@protonmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party SDK, please contact us so we can address it.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as features or SDKs evolve. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
